## Prompt original — três modelos separados

Create an original, cohesive three-item 3D asset pack for a Brazilian Caatinga
village game. Models must be individually downloadable GLB, not one fused scene.
1. A naturalistic Brazilian free-range adult hen: warm chestnut/buff feathers,
fine feather texture and layered wing/tail silhouette, small red comb and wattles,
clear eyes and beak, two separate legs with three forward toes, feet on ground.
2. One yellow/buff chick: soft down suggested by texture, rounded body but clear
beak, eyes and two legs, separate model for several small followers.
3. A lean adult mixed-breed Caatinga goat: short mottled cream/brown coat, natural
horizontal torso, four distinct grounded legs and cloven hooves, modest swept-back
horns, small beard and lateral ears. Anatomically believable, not chibi/cartoon.
All neutral standing poses, anatomically separated limbs, no plinth, no ground,
no scenery, no accessories, no text or brands. Smooth shaded medium-detail
stylized realism suitable for WebGL, approximately6000 triangles per adult,
2000 chick, one material and1K PBR maps each, natural proportions. We will rig
animal locomotion ourselves if quadruped/bird rigging is unavailable. Please do
not apply humanoid animations to these animals. Use only this original written
brief, no third-party mesh or protected character. Supply provenance/model IDs,
actual downloadable formats and the official terms URL governing commercial use
and attribution for these generated outputs. Do not claim a license without a
verifiable official source.
